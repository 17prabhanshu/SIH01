# Research Registry

This registry tracks the foundational scientific literature, papers, and architectural references used in the design of the NER Landslide Early Warning platform.

| **MintPy (Miami INsar Time-series software in PYthon)** | SBAS InSAR Time-series | Algorithms for slow-moving slope deformation. | [10.1016/j.cageo.2019.104331](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2019.104331) |
